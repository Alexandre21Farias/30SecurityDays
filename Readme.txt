# My Journey into DevOps and Cybersecurity

Hi! My name is Alexandre, and I'm currently studying Computer Engineering. I'm working toward transitioning my career into DevOps while deepening my knowledge of backend development, infrastructure, and cybersecurity.

## Why DevOps and Cybersecurity?

Over time, I've realized that my interests are shifting away from traditional software development toward infrastructure, automation, and security.

With the rapid advancement of AI, writing code is becoming increasingly accessible. While this creates many opportunities, it also raises important concerns about software security, vulnerabilities, and the reliability of the systems we build.

I'm particularly interested in understanding how systems work behind the scenes, how applications communicate, how data is protected, and how we can build more secure and reliable infrastructure. I'm also interested in cryptography and the principles behind information security.

## My Goals

My goal is to develop a strong technical foundation in backend development, Linux, networking, DevOps practices, and cybersecurity.

I'm also preparing for public service examinations (*concursos públicos*) in Brazil, with the long-term objective of securing a stable position in the technology field, improving my quality of life, and building a sustainable career in information security.

## What I'm Learning

- **Backend Development:** Understanding APIs, databases, application architecture, and server-side programming.
- **DevOps:** Learning Linux, automation, CI/CD, containers, and infrastructure management.
- **Cybersecurity:** Exploring system security, vulnerabilities, and security best practices.
- **Networking:** Understanding network protocols, communication, and infrastructure.
- **Cryptography:** Studying the fundamentals of encryption and data protection.

## My Vision

I want to move beyond simply writing code and develop a deeper understanding of the systems that run, connect, and protect our applications.

This journey is about building practical skills, understanding technology at a deeper level, and preparing for opportunities that align with my professional interests and long-term goals.

I'm documenting my progress as I learn, experiment, and work toward becoming a professional in DevOps and cybersecurity.
